Irene Adriá Iniesta

Fine Arts student, illustrator and saxophonist. Valencia, Spain.

Right now

I'm in my second year of a Bachelor's degree in Fine Arts at the Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV).

Alongside my studies, I illustrate children's books for the author David James. I illustrated Alba and the Dragon and I'm currently working on a new story in the same spirit.

I also give private lessons, helping primary school students with their schoolwork and with English.

Where I'm headed

I'd love to work in university teaching and research, in art direction, or in artistic creation within a publisher or company.

Next year I'm planning to spend my third year abroad, through Erasmus or PROMOE, ideally somewhere far from Spain. This year I'd also like to start an external course to complement my degree.
